# Phase 4.0 — Competitor Design Audit

**Status:** Research complete  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Method:** Public page review (Omni Calculator, Calculator.net, Calculator Soup, Symbolab landing, Desmos + RapidTables as modern references). No proprietary assets copied.

## Omni Calculator — strengths & weaknesses

**Strengths:** Category taxonomy at scale; search; consistent calculator chrome; professional unit handling; brand trust from volume.

**Weaknesses:** Overwhelming choice; variable step transparency; consumer marketing tone; hard to verify method conventions per tool.

**CalcLume opportunity:** Be the **understandable** alternative for users who outgrow “black box” results — not compete on count.

---

## Calculator.net — strengths & weaknesses

**Strengths:** Extreme topical breadth; fast access for known calculator names; long domain authority.

**Weaknesses:** Visually dated; link-farm navigation; inconsistent depth of explanation; ad density hurts mobile UX.

**CalcLume opportunity:** Modern, accessible layout with **one consistent page contract** across all tools.

---

## Calculator Soup — strengths & weaknesses

**Strengths:** Education-aligned copy; formula visibility in articles; teacher/student familiarity.

**Weaknesses:** Cluttered pages; mixed ad placement; uneven interactive quality.

**CalcLume opportunity:** Match educational intent with **interactive step engines**, not static prose alone.

---

## Symbolab — strengths & weaknesses

**Strengths:** Step-by-step math as core product; strong brand for algebra/calculus.

**Weaknesses:** Paywall for full steps; not a general calculator library; different product category.

**CalcLume opportunity:** Own **statistics, finance, and everyday** transparency niches Symbolab does not serve.

---

## Desmos & RapidTables — modern references

**Desmos:** Minimal UI, tool-first, excellent focus — inspiration for **calculator-before-essay** layout (already CalcLume policy).

**RapidTables:** Conversion tables as content — CalcLume should **not** become a table farm; use tables only inside tool results.

---

## Design opportunities for CalcLume (non-copying)

1. **Category quality gates** — Publish category landings only with ≥3 live tools (see Category Architecture doc).
2. **Unified result chrome** — Same four-block pattern (answer → formula → steps → interpretation) across categories with category-appropriate extensions (e.g. box plot for stats, amortization schedule for finance).
3. **Trust strip** — Compact “Local calculation · Sources · Last reviewed” near result (V2).
4. **Directory search** — Client-side fuzzy search over published index (Phase 4.2, no backend).
5. **No visual chaos** — Resist icon explosion and rainbow category tiles; use typography and restrained accent colors per category.

---

## Explicit non-goals (competitive)

- Do not replicate Omni’s 3,000-tool breadth target.
- Do not adopt Calculator.net link-directory homepage.
- Do not add Symbolab-style paywalls for steps.
- Do not introduce ad slots “because competitors do.”
