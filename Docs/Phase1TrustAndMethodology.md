## Methodology commitments (current)

- Formula selection from documented source hierarchy
- Independent verification against reference examples
- Edge-case testing before publication
- Documented rounding and unit handling
- Public correction process via email
- Separation of current commitments from future aspirations

## Editorial commitments

- Human review before publication
- AI may assist drafting but not replace verification
- No fabricated sources, authors, or credentials
- Calculator logic separated from editorial content
- Curated localization only (no mass translation)

## Privacy commitments (Phase 1)

- No user accounts
- No calculator data transmitted or stored
- No analytics or advertising in Phase 1
- Email contact only; no contact form backend

## Honesty requirements

- Do not invent team members, company history, or expert reviewers
- Do not claim usage statistics or search volumes
- Do not present in-preparation calculators as functional
- Do not promise analytics/ad behavior not yet implemented

## Source hierarchy

1. Government and intergovernmental sources
2. Universities and peer-reviewed publications
3. Standards bodies
4. Established textbooks and reference works
5. Reputable professional organizations

## Correction process

Users report errors to hello@calclume.com. Verified errors are corrected with updated last-reviewed date on affected pages.
