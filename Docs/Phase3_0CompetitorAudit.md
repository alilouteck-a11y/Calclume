# Phase 3.0 — Competitor Audit

**Research date:** 2026-09-01  
**Method:** Live web search + representative page inspection of top calculator results  
**Limitation:** Not a full ranked SERP scrape for every locale/device; judgments are qualitative SERP observations, not verified positions or traffic estimates.

Evidence type legend: **Directional** unless marked otherwise.

## Outlier / IQR family

### Dominant SERP page type
**Interactive calculators** with short educational copy (Directional).

### Representative competitors

| Site | Strengths | Weaknesses / gaps |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Pearson Channels Range & IQR | Steps, method comparison, box plot, outlier fences | Brand-heavy; not CalcLume’s trust/privacy positioning |
| PlotNerd IQR | Clear fences + plot; quartile-method education elsewhere on site | Productized tool suite; mixed depth |
| MetricGate IQR Outlier | Dual 1.5/3.0 fences; EDA framing | Heavier analytics UI; less “show the work” classroom focus |
| standarddeviationcalculator.io Outlier | Simple IQR steps | Thin design; limited method transparency |
| Various thin calculator farms | Fast answers | Ads, weak methodology, unclear quartile rules |

### Google SERP satisfaction
No evidence that Google fully answers outlier listing in a calculator widget for all queries; users still land on tools (Directional).

### High-risk competitors
Pearson / major education brands and polished niche tools. Universities often rank for **definition** queries, less often as best interactive calculator UX (Directional).

### CalcLume opportunity
Win on: explicit quartile convention, full working, fence table, accessible visualization, local-only privacy, links to MAD.

## Five-number summary family

### Dominant SERP page type
Dedicated five-number-summary calculators; many also add IQR, outliers, and box plots (Directional).

### Representative competitors

| Site | Notes |
|------|-------|
| 5numbersummary.com | Method options; box-plot framing |
| CalculatorSoup | Steps + textbook-style exclusive halves |
| StatsUnlock / StatisticsFundamentals | Bundle five-number + IQR + fences + plot |
| AtoZMath / StatisticsCalculators.net | Thin step tools |

### Implication
Publishing a bare five-number-summary page **without** IQR/outliers would under-serve SERP expectations and invite cannibalization with an Outlier/IQR page.

## Coefficient of variation

### Dominant SERP page type
Calculators + education sites (BYJU’S, Inch Calculator, MetricGate, StatsUnlock) (Directional).

### Gaps CalcLume can fill
- Sample vs population SD clarity
- Percentage vs unitless ratio display
- Explicit “undefined / misleading near zero mean” warnings
- Neutral interpretation (no arbitrary “good CV” thresholds without context)

### Risk
Finance/lab audiences may expect extras (RSD, CI) that expand scope.

---

## Standard error

### Dominant SERP page type
SEM calculators (Omni, Statistics by Jim, StatCalc, Calculator-Online) (Directional).

### Gaps
- Strict SEM vs “standard error of measurement” disambiguation
- Raw-data and summary (s, n) modes
- Clear contrast vs standard deviation (ties to MAD cluster carefully)

### Risk
Inference cluster competitors are mature; differentiation must be pedagogical excellence.

---

## Critical values

### Dominant SERP page type
Multi-distribution calculators and hubs (Omni, Social Science Statistics, Stat Study Hub, Statistics by Jim) (Directional).

### Gaps
Possible for a tightly scoped z/t first release — but incomplete vs hub expectations.

### Risk
High implementation and interpretation complexity for a **second** launch; weaker link to MAD.

## Bottom line

Competition is real but often **thin or convention-opaque**. CalcLume’s best entry is the combined EDA tool where transparency of quartile method and accessible visualization are rare together.
