# Phase 4.0 — Product Positioning

**Status:** Locked for Phase 4.1 planning  
**Date:** 2026-09-02

## Product-positioning statement

**CalcLume is an English-first calculator library for people who want answers they can verify.** Every tool shows the formula, the working steps, and a plain-language interpretation — with calculations that run locally in the browser and data that never leaves the device.

## Homepage value proposition (short)

**Clear calculators that show the work** — formulas, steps, and interpretation on every page, without ads or clutter.

## Brand promise

When you use CalcLume, you will see **how** a number was produced, **what** it means, and **which** conventions were applied — not just a result in a box.

## Differentiation pillars

1. **Transparent working** — Result, formula, steps, and interpretation are first-class UI, not buried FAQ text.
2. **Local and private** — No accounts, no transmitted inputs, no analytics-driven profiling of calculator usage (current posture).
3. **Method honesty** — Quartile methods, units, and limitations are stated explicitly; sources are cited where claims matter.
4. **Calm, focused interface** — No ad slots, pop-ups, or visual noise typical of calculator farms.
5. **Editorial discipline** — No indexable empty categories, no keyword-split pages, no fabricated volumes or credentials.

## Why users choose CalcLume over generic calculator sites

| User need | Generic farm | CalcLume |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Verify a homework or work result | Result only; steps often missing | Step-by-step working + formula |
| Understand conventions (e.g. quartile method) | Hidden or inconsistent | Selector + documented method |
| Privacy-sensitive data | Unknown third-party scripts | Local-only calculation (documented) |
| Mobile use amid ads | Heavy banners, layout shift | Static, restrained layout |
| Trust for coursework or reporting | Anonymous blog formulas | Methodology, sources, last-reviewed dates |

## What we do not claim

- Not the largest calculator library.
- Not the “most accurate” site on the internet.
- Not a substitute for professional financial, medical, or legal advice.
- Not AI-generated answers without verifiable math.

## Audience segments

| Segment | Primary need | CalcLume fit |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Students | Learn and verify steps | Strong |
| Analysts / researchers | Transparent EDA statistics | Strong (current) |
| Business users | Quick defensible numbers | Medium (future finance/business) |
| Everyday users | Practical converters and date math | Medium (future everyday/conversions) |
| Professionals (health, construction) | Code-compliant formulas | Requires YMYL rigor per tool |

## Scalability principle

Grow breadth **by category clusters**, not by isolated keyword pages. Each new category earns a landing page only when it has enough published tools to justify user intent — not to capture empty SERP shells.
